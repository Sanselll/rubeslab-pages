Privacy Policy

Rube's Lab

Effective date: February 15, 2026

Bouland Games ("we", "our", "us") developed Rube's Lab as a mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

Rube's Lab does not require account creation or login. We do not operate our own servers to store your personal data. However, certain information may be collected through third-party services integrated into the app:

  • Advertising identifiers — Your device's Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS) may be collected by our advertising partners to serve ads.
  • Device information — Device model, operating system version, language settings, and screen resolution may be collected for analytics and ad delivery.
  • Gameplay analytics — Anonymous gameplay data such as levels completed and session duration may be collected to help us improve the game.
  • Purchase transactions — When you make in-app purchases, transaction data is processed by Google Play or Apple App Store. We do not store your payment information.

2. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services that may collect data according to their own privacy policies:

  • Unity Analytics — For gameplay analytics and crash reporting. Unity Privacy Policy
  • Unity LevelPlay / IronSource — For serving advertisements. IronSource Privacy Policy
  • Google Play / Apple App Store — For processing in-app purchases. See their respective privacy policies.

3. How We Use Information

Information collected through third-party services is used for:

  • Displaying advertisements, including personalized ads based on your interests
  • Analyzing gameplay to improve the game experience
  • Processing in-app purchase transactions
  • Fraud prevention and security

4. Data Storage

Your game progress and settings are stored locally on your device. This data is removed when you uninstall the app. Data collected by third-party services is stored according to their respective data retention policies.

5. Your Rights and Choices

  • Opt out of personalized ads — You can opt out by adjusting your device settings: on Android, go to Settings > Google > Ads; on iOS, go to Settings > Privacy > Tracking.
  • Reset advertising identifier — You can reset your advertising ID through your device settings at any time.
  • Data deletion — To request deletion of any data associated with you, contact us at boulandgames@gmail.com.

6. Children's Privacy

Rube's Lab is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the app, please contact us at boulandgames@gmail.com and we will take steps to delete such information.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
